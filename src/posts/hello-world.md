Writing has always been the way I process things. Not to perform clarity, but to find it.

This space is for that. No particular format, no consistent cadence. Just things I want to think through — about communities, coordination, building online, and occasionally something that has nothing to do with any of those.

## What this is

I work at the intersection of community operations and product. Most of what I do lives in the quiet infrastructure of large online spaces: moderation systems, support flows, event rhythms, the unglamorous work of keeping a space healthy at scale.

Writing here is a way to make some of that thinking visible. Not a portfolio of achievements — more like notes from inside the work.

## What this isn't

A newsletter. A thread dump. A personal brand exercise.

I'm not here to build an audience or maintain a content calendar. If something is worth writing, I'll write it. If not, I won't.

## A few things I'll probably write about

- Community dynamics at scale — how consensus forms, how it breaks, how you repair it
- The texture of moderation work that never makes it into a job description
- Web3 communities specifically: what's different, what's the same, what's genuinely new
- Tools and workflows that actually changed how I work
- Occasional observations that don't fit anywhere else
